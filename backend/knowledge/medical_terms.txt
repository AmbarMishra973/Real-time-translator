# Medical & Healthcare Terms Knowledge Base for AI Translator & RAG

Hypertension:
Abnormally high blood pressure in the arteries, commonly defined as blood pressure reading above 130/80 mmHg.

Diagnosis:
The identification of the nature of an illness or other health problem by examination of physical symptoms, lab tests, and medical history.

Prescription:
A formal written instruction by a qualified medical practitioner authorizing a patient to be provided a medicine or treatment.

Triage:
The assignment of degrees of urgency to wounds or illnesses to decide the order of treatment of a large number of patients or casualties.

Outpatient:
A patient who attends a hospital or clinic for medical consultation or treatment without staying overnight.

Prognosis:
The likely course, progression, and outcome of a medical condition or disease.

Contraindication:
A specific situation or condition in which a drug, procedure, or surgery should not be used because it may be harmful to the patient.

Chronic Disease:
A human health condition or disease that is persistent or otherwise long-lasting in its effects, typically lasting 3 months or more.
