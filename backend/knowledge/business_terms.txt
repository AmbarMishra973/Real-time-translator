# Business & Corporate Terms Knowledge Base for AI Translator & RAG

ROI:
Return on Investment. A financial metric used to evaluate the profitability or efficiency of an investment relative to its cost. Usually kept as 'ROI' in corporate discourse.

Sprint:
A set period of time (typically 1 to 4 weeks) during which specific work or software features must be completed and made ready for review in Agile methodologies.

KPI:
Key Performance Indicator. A quantifiable measure of performance over time for a specific objective, team, or project milestone.

Stakeholders:
Individuals, teams, or groups that have an interest in or are affected by the outcome of a business initiative, product, or project.

Deliverables:
Tangible or intangible goods, software features, or services that must be provided upon the completion of a project or milestone.

Quarterly Review:
A formal evaluation meeting conducted every three months to assess financial performance, project progress, and strategic company targets.

Bandwidth:
In business terminology, the capacity or availability of an individual or team to take on additional tasks, responsibilities, or projects.

Scope Creep:
The uncontrolled expansion or continuous growth in a project's scope or requirements without adjustments to time, cost, and resources.

Burn Rate:
The rate at which a startup or company spends its venture capital or cash reserves before generating positive cash flow from operations.

Synergy:
The combined interaction or cooperation of two or more organizations, teams, or substances to produce a combined effect greater than the sum of their separate effects.
